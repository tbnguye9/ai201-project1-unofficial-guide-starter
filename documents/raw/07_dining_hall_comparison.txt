Title: We Ate at Every Dining Hall for a Week — Here's Our Verdict
Authors: Campus Food Review Team

We sent three student reviewers to eat at Main Hall, South Cafe, and North 
Grill every day for a full week. Here's what we found.

---

Main Hall

Variety: 9/10 — The most diverse menu by far. Seven distinct stations including 
international, comfort food, deli, salad bar, pizza, pasta, and dessert. 
Something for everyone, though quality varies by station.

Consistency: 6/10 — Hit or miss depending on the day and time. Thursday evening 
is consistently the best meal of the week. Monday lunches are often disappointing.

Wait Times: 4/10 — The biggest weakness. Average wait of 18 minutes during peak 
hours. The app helps but doesn't solve the fundamental capacity problem.

Cleanliness: 8/10 — Tables are cleared quickly, floors are mopped regularly. 
The serving area could use more attention during rush hours.

---

South Cafe

Variety: 6/10 — Limited hot food, strong grab-and-go section. Better coffee 
program than anywhere else on campus. The pastry selection changes daily.

Consistency: 8/10 — Most reliable dining option. You always know what you're 
getting. Staff turnover is lower here so regulars recognize you.

Wait Times: 9/10 — Rarely more than 5 minutes. The grab-and-go section means 
zero wait if you plan ahead.

Cleanliness: 9/10 — Best maintained of all three. Noticeably cleaner than Main Hall.

---

North Grill

Variety: 5/10 — Intentionally limited to burgers, bowls, and a few sides. 
What it does, it does well. Not for everyday dining if you need variety.

Consistency: 9/10 — Most consistent food quality on campus. The menu doesn't 
change much which means the cooks have perfected their few items.

Wait Times: 7/10 — Moderate waits, manageable. Custom orders take longer.

Cleanliness: 7/10 — Good but not great. Grill areas can get smoky.
