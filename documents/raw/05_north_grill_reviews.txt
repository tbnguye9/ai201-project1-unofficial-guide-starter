Review 1 (Rating: 5/5, Username: burgerlover_ben)
Best burgers on campus, period. They smash the patties fresh to order and the 
buns are toasted. Takes about 8 minutes but it's worth it. The sweet potato fries 
are a must-get. This place is criminally undervisited because it's off the main path.

---

Review 2 (Rating: 4/5, Username: athlete_eats)
Perfect post-workout meal location. High protein options, good portions, and the 
staff knows the regulars. The grilled chicken bowl has like 45g of protein and 
tastes actually seasoned, not bland. Only complaint: closes too early at 8pm.

---

Review 3 (Rating: 3/5, Username: plant_based_pete)
Not great for vegetarians. The veggie option is usually just a black bean burger 
which is fine but uninspired. The sides are mostly fries. If you eat meat this 
place is great, if not, look elsewhere.

---

Review 4 (Rating: 5/5, Username: study_break_snacker)
The nachos here are unironically one of the best late-afternoon snacks on campus. 
Loaded with toppings, not just chips with sad salsa. The portion is also huge — 
I've split it with a friend before. Highly recommend as a study break treat.

---

Review 5 (Rating: 4/5, Username: weekend_warrior_wren)
Open on weekends which is more than I can say for some places. Weekend brunch 
menu is different from weekdays — more breakfast-style items. The french toast 
sticks sound childish but they're genuinely great. Solid spot.
