Review 1 (Rating: 5/5, Username: coffeeAddict_joel)
The cold brew here is genuinely good — not the watery stuff you get at the main 
hall. A bit pricey at $4.50 but worth it before an 8am lecture. The breakfast 
sandwiches are made fresh and the portions are solid.

---

Review 2 (Rating: 3/5, Username: lateNightStudy)
Only place open after 9pm which is the main reason I come here. Limited menu at 
night — basically just sandwiches and snacks. Microwaved food tastes like it. 
Would give 4 stars if they kept the hot food going longer.

---

Review 3 (Rating: 4/5, Username: quick_between_class)
Perfect for grabbing something fast between classes. The grab-and-go section is 
well stocked until about 2pm. After that it gets picked over. Salads are fresh 
and the wraps are actually filling.

---

Review 4 (Rating: 2/5, Username: gluten_free_struggle)
Almost nothing here for gluten-free students. I asked staff about ingredients and 
they weren't sure about cross-contamination. For students with allergies, this 
place is risky. They should label things better.

---

Review 5 (Rating: 4/5, Username: budget_student_eats)
Most affordable option on campus. The combo deal — entree plus drink for $7 — 
is the best value you'll find. Food is basic but consistent. Never had a bad 
experience, just not exciting.
