Title: 20 Dining Hall Tips From Students Who Learned the Hard Way
Compiled from: Senior student survey, 200 respondents

---

Tip 1: Download the dining app before your first day. It shows today's menu 
at every location and live wait times. This alone will save you hours of 
walking to a hall only to find it packed or serving something you don't like.

Tip 2: The best meal of the week at Main Hall is Thursday dinner. 
They save the premium proteins for Thursday. Students call it "Fancy Thursday."

Tip 3: Never go to Main Hall between noon and 1:15pm unless you enjoy 
standing in line with 300 other people. Adjust your class schedule if possible 
to eat at 11:30am or after 1:30pm.

Tip 4: Meal swipes expire weekly, dining dollars don't. Track your swipes. 
Most people waste 5-10 swipes per semester.

Tip 5: The to-go container is your best friend. One swipe, more food.

Tip 6: For the freshest food, arrive right when a station opens after 
a reset — 11am, 5pm, and 9pm are the reset times at Main Hall.

Tip 7: South Cafe has the best coffee on campus and it's included in 
your dining dollars. Skip the Starbucks.

Tip 8: North Grill burgers are made-to-order fresh. Worth the extra 
walk from East campus. Not worth the walk from West campus in rain.

Tip 9: Register with dining services if you have dietary restrictions 
before the semester starts, not after. The process takes a week.

Tip 10: The Sunday night closing rush at Main Hall is actually great — 
they're trying to clear out perishables so portions get bigger and they 
add bonus items. Peak hours but peak value.

Tip 11: The nutrition app shows full macros. If you're an athlete or 
tracking food, this is essential. Takes 5 minutes to set up.

Tip 12: You can eat outside. The area behind Main Hall has tables and 
is almost never crowded. Way better than fighting for an indoor seat.

Tip 13: Dining hall staff remember kind students. Being polite to staff 
has gotten many students extra portions and early access to fresh batches.

Tip 14: The waffle iron at Main Hall breakfast is self-serve. This 
information should be in the admissions brochure.

Tip 15: Bring a reusable water bottle. All dining halls have filtered 
water stations — saves you $3/day vs buying bottled water.
