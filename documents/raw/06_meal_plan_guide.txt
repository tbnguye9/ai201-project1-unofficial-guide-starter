Title: Everything You Need to Know About the Meal Plan (That They Don't Tell You)
Author: Sarah K., Senior, Computer Science

After four years of navigating the dining system, here's what I wish someone 
had told me freshman year.

---

Understanding Your Balance

You have two separate balances: Meal Swipes and Dining Dollars. Swipes are 
used at the three main dining halls (Main Hall, South Cafe, North Grill). 
Dining Dollars work like cash everywhere on campus including food trucks, 
the convenience store, and the coffee carts.

Critical rule: Meal Swipes expire every Sunday at midnight. Dining Dollars 
roll over semester to semester but not year to year. If you have leftover 
swipes on Sunday night, use them to grab food to stockpile for your dorm room.

---

Best Value Strategy

The to-go container swipe is the single best value on campus. One swipe gets 
you a large container you can stuff with as much food as fits. Students who 
know about this eat much better per-swipe than those who eat in.

Dining Dollars go furthest at the campus food trucks, which are typically 
$2-3 cheaper than equivalent items inside the dining halls.

---

Avoiding the Freshman 15

The salad bar at Main Hall and the grain bowls at South Cafe are the two 
healthiest consistent options. Avoid the late-night pizza and waffle stations 
if you're trying to eat well — they're strategically placed to tempt you.

The nutrition app (search "CampusEats" in your app store) shows full macros 
for everything served. It's accurate and updated daily.

---

Peak Hours to Avoid

Worst times to go: Monday-Friday 12:00-1:15pm at Main Hall. Saturday-Sunday 
10:30am-12:30pm at North Grill (brunch rush). South Cafe is most crowded 
8:00-9:30am on weekdays.

Best times: Any dining hall at 5pm sharp when they reset the hot bar. 
Everything is freshest right after a reset.
