For all my fellow night owls and late-night studiers — a comprehensive 
guide to not starving after the dining halls close.

---

What's Open and When

South Cafe: Open until 11pm Sunday-Thursday, 12am Friday-Saturday. 
Limited menu after 9pm (sandwiches, snacks, drinks). Coffee until close.

Campus Convenience Store (Library Basement): Open 24/7. Accepts dining 
dollars. Has microwaveable meals, instant noodles, cereal, fruit, snacks. 
Not a full meal but works in a pinch. The $3.99 mac and cheese cups are 
a campus staple at 2am.

Food Trucks: Three trucks rotate on campus. Check the CampusFoodTrucks 
app for daily locations and hours. Usually available until 10pm weekdays. 
The Noodle Truck and the Taco Truck are student favorites.

Vending Machines: Every dorm has them, accepts dining dollars. Limited 
to chips and candy — emergency use only.

---

Best Late-Night Strategies

The to-go box strategy: Before Main Hall closes at 9pm, use a swipe 
for a to-go box and pack it with foods that keep well — salad items, 
sandwiches, fruit. Eat it during your late study session.

Dining dollar arbitrage: Dining dollars go further at food trucks than 
dining halls. A full meal from the Taco Truck runs $8-10 vs $12-15 worth 
of swipe value at a dining hall.

Sunday prep: Dining swipes expire Sunday night. Load up a to-go box with 
snacks and meal-prep items Sunday evening to start the week right.
