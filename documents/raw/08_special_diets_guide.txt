Title: Navigating Dining Halls with Dietary Restrictions
Author: Multiple student contributors

This guide was compiled from experiences of students with various dietary 
needs — vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free, and common allergies.

---

Vegetarian and Vegan Options

Main Hall has the most consistent vegan options with a dedicated station 
that rotates daily. The vegan station is marked with a green V sign. 
Quality varies — the grain bowls are excellent, the vegan "meat" dishes 
are generally disappointing.

South Cafe labels vegan items clearly on the grab-and-go section. About 
30% of items are vegan. The overnight oats and fruit cups are always vegan.

North Grill has the weakest plant-based selection. The black bean burger 
is the only dedicated option. Ask staff for off-menu substitutions — 
they can usually do a veggie bowl with notice.

---

Halal and Kosher

Halal: One dedicated station at Main Hall, open daily 11am-2pm and 5pm-8pm. 
Runs out fast — arrive within the first 30 minutes of service. No halal 
options at South Cafe or North Grill. Off-campus options: Halal truck on 
University Ave parks outside the library Monday-Friday.

Kosher: Limited options. A small kosher section at Main Hall carries 
pre-packaged certified kosher meals — must be requested at the service 
desk 24 hours in advance. Cost is covered by meal swipe.

---

Gluten-Free

This is the most challenging restriction on campus. Cross-contamination is 
a real risk at all three locations. For celiac students: contact dining 
services directly at dining@university.edu to arrange a safe meal protocol. 
They will prepare a dedicated meal in a separate kitchen upon 48-hour notice.

For gluten-sensitive (not celiac): the salad bar at Main Hall is the safest 
option if you stick to whole vegetables and avoid croutons. The rice station 
is generally safe but ask about sauces.

---

Allergy Awareness

All dining locations have allergy binders at the service desk listing 
ingredients for every dish. Ask staff to see it — they're required to 
show you. The CampusEats app also lists the top 9 allergens for each item.

If you have a severe allergy, register with the dining services office 
before the semester starts. They can flag your account so staff are alerted.
