Review 1 (Rating: 4/5, Username: hungryCS_student)
The pasta bar is the hidden gem of this place. Always fresh, staff refills it 
regularly. Avoid going between 12-1pm though — the line wraps around the building. 
Best time is 11:30am or after 1:30pm.

---

Review 2 (Rating: 2/5, Username: freshman2023)
Food quality dropped a lot this semester. The salad bar is almost always out of 
protein toppings by noon. They really need to restock more often. The only reliable 
thing is the cereal station at breakfast.

---

Review 3 (Rating: 3/5, Username: veggieVibes)
Vegetarian options are limited to one station. As someone who doesn't eat meat, 
I end up eating the same thing every day. The veggie burger is okay but they only 
serve it on Wednesdays. Wish they had more variety.

---

Review 4 (Rating: 5/5, Username: mealplan_master)
Best dining hall on campus, no contest. The made-to-order egg station at breakfast 
is worth waking up early for. Friendly staff, always clean. The dessert section 
has improved a lot since last year.

---

Review 5 (Rating: 1/5, Username: ramen_or_bust)
Waited 25 minutes for a burger during lunch rush. By the time I got it, I only 
had 10 minutes to eat before class. They need more staff during peak hours. 
The food itself was fine but the wait is unbearable.
